# Vision-Language-Models

### 1. Industrial Machinery Detection and Monitoring
- Uses **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** to identify excavators and dump trucks in video streams.
- Extracts machinery attributes, activity status, and location using predefined Regions of Interest (ROIs).

### 2. Bottle Label and Damage Inspection
- Combines **YOLO tracking** and **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** for automated bottle inspection.
- Analyzes detected bottles to identify missing labels and visible damage.

### 3. Cash Counter Theft Monitoring
- Uses a **Vision-Language Model (VLM)** to analyze surveillance footage for potentially suspicious activities at cash counters.
- Generates structured observations and descriptions of relevant visual evidence.

### 4. Vehicle Detection and Attribute Recognition
- Integrates **YOLO12** object detection and tracking with **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL**.
- Identifies vehicle types, colors, and manufacturers within predefined monitoring zones.

### 5. Vision-Language-Based Car Accident Analysis
- Combines **YOLO tracking** and **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** to analyze road surveillance footage.
- Assesses potential accidents, including severity, vehicles involved, and possible causes.
